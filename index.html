    Frequently asked questions

    Is this text to speech tool really free?

    Yes. The free plan lets you play unlimited text, up to 2,000 characters at a time, using the voices installed on your device.

    Why do the voices differ between devices?

    Voices come from your operating system and browser (for example Windows, macOS, Android or Chrome's online voices), so the list and quality vary.

    How does audio export work?

    Browsers can't hand over the raw audio of built-in voices, so export records this tab's audio while the text plays, trims silence, then converts it to WAV or MP3. It works best on desktop Chrome or Edge.

    Can I use the audio commercially?

    System voices may carry their own licenses. For guaranteed commercial rights, choose the Pro plan with AI voices.
